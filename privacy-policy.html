Privacy Policy — elAutomation

Last updated: 28 August 2026

elAutomation ("we," "our," or "us") provides an automated content scheduling and publishing tool ("Service") that helps users create and publish short-form video content to their own TikTok account. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use elAutomation and connect your TikTok account, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your TikTok username, user ID, and profile information provided through TikTok's OAuth authorization.
  • Content data: Video files, captions, hashtags, and scheduling data that you provide or that our system generates from approved templates for publishing to your TikTok account.
  • Authorization tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used solely to publish content on your behalf.
  • Usage data: Logs related to scheduling, publishing status, and errors, used for troubleshooting and service improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Authenticate and connect to your TikTok account via TikTok's official API.
  • Generate and publish video content to your TikTok account according to your schedule.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of our automation workflow.
  • Communicate with you regarding service status or issues.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Access tokens and account data are stored securely and are only accessible to authorized systems required to operate the Service.
  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Section 6).

4. Third-Party Services

Our Service may rely on third-party infrastructure (e.g., automation platforms, video rendering services) to operate. These providers only process data as necessary to perform their function within our workflow and are not authorized to use your data for any other purpose.

5. TikTok API Compliance

Our use of information received from TikTok's API complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and TikTok's Platform Terms. We only request the minimum permissions (scopes) necessary to provide the Service.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Disconnect your TikTok account from our Service at any time via TikTok's app permissions settings.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us (see Section 8).

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
